## 4. Concept of the day: rules before results
If you look at results first and then pick the rules, you will find rules that fit the past by luck. That is overfitting. Our fix: a card's rules are written and committed before the first backtest, and then frozen. Today the skeptic applied it: with no H004/H005 card there were no frozen rules, so it would not review anything. Our BTC bot shows the cost of ignoring it: the best 2021-23 settings had profit factor 2.09 but only 0.69 on 2024+ (INDEX.md, C005).
