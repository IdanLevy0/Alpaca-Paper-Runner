## 4. Concept of the day: statistical power (small samples)
Power is how likely a test is to detect an edge that is really there. H004 will fire only on rare gap days. With few trades, one lucky or unlucky day can swing the average. A "PASS" or "FAIL" on that sample says little. H001 shows the other side: it had many trades and beat 94% of random picks, so we could tell the edge was real but too small. Costs of about 4 bp/day ate it. That is why the Skeptic asks for the trade count first. Fewer trades means a wider margin of error on the result.
